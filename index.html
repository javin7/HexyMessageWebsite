XianTao (Carol) Luo

Registered Massage Therapist (RMT) · Registered Acupuncturist (R.Ac) · Registered Traditional Chinese Medicine Practitioner (R.TCM.P) · Osteopathic Manual Practitioner

Carol Luo is a multidisciplinary healthcare practitioner with a background in Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), acupuncture, registered massage therapy, and osteopathic manual therapy. She brings 20 years of clinical experience in China, including 10 years in general hospitals and 10 years in maternal and child healthcare hospitals.

Full biographyClose biography

After moving to Canada, Carol continued her professional training at Canadian medical and traditional Chinese medicine institutions, as well as the National Academy of Osteopathy. She has also served as a student supervisor at John & Jenny Traditional Chinese Medicine College for four years.

Influenced by Traditional Chinese Medicine from an early age, Carol combines classical TCM principles with contemporary clinical knowledge. Her approach emphasizes gentle, balanced, and individualized care, drawing on acupuncture, herbal medicine, cupping, gua sha, moxibustion, tuina, and manual therapy as appropriate to each patient.

Her clinical interests include musculoskeletal pain and mobility concerns, headaches, sleep and digestive concerns, women’s health, and postpartum wellness. Carol is committed to attentive, patient-centered care that supports comfort, functional well-being, and each individual’s health goals.

Joe Wu

Osteopathic Manual Practitioner

Joe Wu is an osteopathic manual practitioner and a graduate of the National Academy of Osteopathy (NAO), where he completed a Diploma of Osteopathy and more than 1,000 hours of supervised hands-on clinical training. He holds professional memberships with the College of Registered Manual Osteopaths (CRMO) and the ACMA Association.

Full biographyClose biography

Joe focuses on hands-on, individualized care for musculoskeletal discomfort, restricted mobility, postural concerns, and functional recovery. His training includes clinical assessment, soft tissue techniques, joint mobilization, muscle energy techniques, and other gentle manual approaches designed to support mobility and overall physical well-being.

With a compassionate and patient-centered approach, Joe takes time to understand each client’s concerns, assess their needs, and develop an appropriate treatment plan. He strives to create a comfortable, respectful treatment experience while helping clients work toward improved movement, reduced discomfort, and long-term wellness.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about treatments, billing, and appointments.

How does Direct Insurance Billing work?
Bring your insurance card and government ID. We submit your claim electronically at checkout. Your plan determines the covered amount and any remaining balance.
Are massage therapy treatments performed with clothing on?
Standard, prenatal, moxa stick and energy pack massage therapy are performed with clothing on.
How do I book or reschedule?
Click any 'Book Appointment' button to choose your time slot. Your confirmation email contains a direct link to reschedule up to 24 hours in advance.
Do I need a doctor's referral?
A doctor's referral is not required to receive treatment, though some specific insurance plans may require one for reimbursement.
