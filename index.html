Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know

Have questions about treatments, insurance billing, or scheduling? Here are quick answers.

How does Direct Insurance Billing work at BEST LIFE 和熙元?
Simply bring your insurance policy card or member number and government photo ID to your appointment. At checkout, we submit your claim electronically via Telus Health or eClaims. If your plan covers 100%, you pay $0 out of pocket. If there is a co-pay, you only pay the remainder.
What is the difference between RMT Swedish Massage and Chinese Tuina?
Swedish Massage focuses on anatomical muscles, tendons, and circulation using long gliding strokes and kneading with oil. Chinese Tuina (推拿) focuses on traditional meridian channels, acupressure points, and joint mobilizing techniques to stimulate Qi flow and relieve deep internal blockages. Both can be performed by our licensed RMTs.
How do I book or reschedule via Acuity Scheduling?
Click any 'Book Now' button on this website to open our Acuity booking portal. You can select your service, practitioner, and time slot. When you book, you receive an instant confirmation email containing a direct link to change or reschedule your appointment up to 24 hours in advance.
Do I need a doctor's referral for massage therapy?
You do not need a prescription or referral to receive treatment at our clinic. However, a few specific private insurance policies require a doctor's note for reimbursement. We recommend checking your insurance benefits booklet prior to your visit.
What is your cancellation and arrival policy?
We kindly ask for at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling so we may accommodate other clients on our waitlist. We recommend arriving 5–10 minutes before your scheduled start time to relax and complete your confidential health intake form.
